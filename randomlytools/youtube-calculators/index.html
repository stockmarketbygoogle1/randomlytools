How to Choose the Right YouTube Calculator

Use the YouTube Money Calculator for general long-form earnings and income goals. If you already know your channel revenue and views, calculate your actual RPM with the YouTube RPM Calculator. For Shorts, use the YouTube Shorts Earnings Calculator because Shorts use a different monetization model. Country-specific pages provide localized planning examples for India and Iceland.

How YouTube Earnings Are Estimated

A simple creator-side planning formula is estimated revenue = views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. Actual YouTube revenue can vary because RPM depends on audience, monetization, content, advertiser demand, seasonality and other factors. Use your own YouTube Studio RPM when available.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does YouTube pay for 1,000 views?

There is no universal fixed amount. The result depends on your actual RPM and channel conditions.

How much can 1 million YouTube views make?

Multiply 1,000,000 by your RPM and divide by 1,000. For example, a $5 RPM gives a mathematical estimate of $5,000.

Are Shorts earnings calculated the same as long-form?

No. Shorts use a different monetization and revenue-sharing model, so use the dedicated Shorts calculator.
