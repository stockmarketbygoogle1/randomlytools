How We Can Help

RandomlyTools is built to provide simple, useful online tools for everyday tasks. If something is not working as expected, if you have found an error, or if you have an idea that could make a tool more useful, you can contact us by email.

For faster help, please mention the name or URL of the tool you are contacting us about and briefly describe the issue. Screenshots or example inputs can also be helpful when reporting a technical problem.

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Bug reports: Tell us about broken buttons, calculation issues, display problems, or other technical errors.
  • Tool suggestions: Suggest a new calculator, generator, converter, or other useful online utility.
  • Content corrections: Let us know if you find a spelling, wording, dataset, or factual issue on a page.
  • Usability feedback: Share ideas that could make our tools easier to understand or use on desktop and mobile devices.
  • Privacy questions: Contact us if you have questions about how information is handled on the website.
  • Business and partnership inquiries: For relevant collaboration, publishing, or business-related questions, you can contact us by email.

What to Include in Your Message

When reporting a problem, please include enough information for us to understand and reproduce it. Useful details may include the tool name, the page URL, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and the browser or device you were using.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information in a support email.

Response Time

We aim to review contact requests within 48 to 72 business hours. Some requests may take longer when they require technical investigation, content verification, or additional review.

Contacting us does not guarantee that a suggested feature will be added or that a reported issue can be resolved immediately, but useful feedback helps us improve RandomlyTools over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I request a custom random generator tool?

Yes! We actively expand our collection based on real user requests. Send us an email detailing the specific inputs, filters, and use case for the tool you would like to see.

Are the generated results free to use?

Yes, all outputs generated by RandomlyTools are 100% free for both personal and commercial inspiration.

How do I report a technical problem?

Email us with the tool name or page URL, a short description of the problem, and what you expected to happen. If possible, include the browser or device you were using.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. Tell us what the tool should do, what inputs it would need, and how you would use it. We review suggestions when planning future improvements.

Can I report an incorrect result or piece of content?

Yes. Please send the page or tool name, explain the issue, and provide the correct information or an example when available.

How long does it take to receive a response?

We aim to review contact requests within 48 to 72 business hours. More complex technical or content issues may take longer to investigate.

Can I contact RandomlyTools about privacy?

Yes. Privacy-related questions can be sent to the contact email above. You can also review our Privacy Policy for more information.

Do you guarantee that every suggested tool will be added?

No. Suggestions are reviewed based on usefulness, feasibility, and other site considerations. Sending a suggestion does not guarantee that it will be implemented.

How do I report an incorrect cultural name or meaning?

We aim to keep curated datasets useful and accurate. If you notice a typographical, cultural, or linguistic issue, please email us with the correction and any helpful reference or context.
