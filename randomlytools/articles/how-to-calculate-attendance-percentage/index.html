How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

A simple guide to the attendance formula, 75% attendance, and calculating classes to attend or miss.

Attendance Percentage Formula

The basic formula is:

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

For example, if you attended 60 classes out of 80 total classes:

(60 ÷ 80) × 100 = 75%

How to Check 75% Attendance

If your school or college requires 75% attendance, compare your current percentage with 75%. At 75% or higher, you are meeting that target. Below 75%, you need additional attended classes to raise the percentage.

You can use the Attendance Calculator to calculate this instantly and see how many classes you may be able to miss or need to attend.

How Many Classes Can You Miss?

When your attendance is above the target, future absences increase the total number of classes while your attended count stays the same. The maximum number of classes you can miss depends on your current numbers and target percentage.

For example, with 80 attended out of 100 classes, your attendance is 80%. The exact number of additional classes you can miss while remaining at or above 75% can be calculated from the target rather than guessed.

How Many Classes Do You Need to Attend?

If you are below 75%, attending future classes without missing them can gradually increase your percentage. The required number depends on your current attended and total classes.

The calculator can solve this for you, which is especially useful when you are planning the remaining weeks of a semester.

Important Tip

Attendance rules differ between schools, colleges, universities, and courses. A calculator gives the mathematical result, but your institution's official attendance policy should always be treated as the final rule.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the easiest way to calculate attendance?

Divide classes attended by total classes and multiply by 100.

What does 75% attendance mean?

It means you attended 75 out of every 100 classes on average. The actual count can vary, but the ratio must be at least 75%.

Can I calculate a custom attendance target?
