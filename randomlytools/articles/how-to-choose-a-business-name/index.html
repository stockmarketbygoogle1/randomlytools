How to Choose a Business Name

A strong business name should be memorable, relevant to your idea, and practical to use across your brand.

1. Start With Your Business Idea

Think about what you sell, who you serve, and the feeling you want the brand to create. A shop, restaurant, software company, and local service may need very different naming styles.

2. Make It Memorable

Short, pronounceable names are often easier for customers to remember and recommend. Avoid unnecessary complexity when a simpler option communicates the idea well.

3. Think About the Future

Do not choose a name that limits you to one product if you expect the business to expand. A broader brand name can give you more flexibility later.

4. Generate Many Options

Instead of trying to find the perfect name immediately, create a longlist and then remove weak options. The Random Company & Brand Name Generator can help you brainstorm starting points.

5. Check Availability

Before using a name commercially, check the relevant business-name records, trademarks, domain availability, and social handles for your market. A generated name is only an idea until you verify that you can legally and practically use it.
