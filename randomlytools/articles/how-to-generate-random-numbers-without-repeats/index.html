How to Generate Random Numbers Without Repeats

Sometimes you need random numbers, but every result must be unique. This is called sampling without replacement or no-repeat random selection. It is useful for games, assignments, numbered prizes, quizzes, and drawing unique entries.

What does “without repeats” mean?

If you choose from 1 to 20 without repeats, once 7 is selected, 7 cannot appear again in that same selection set. The result contains unique values only.

How to do it

  1. Choose the minimum and maximum number.
  2. Decide how many unique numbers you need.
  3. Use a no-repeat random selection option, if available.
  4. Check that the requested quantity does not exceed the number of possible values.

Example

Suppose you need 5 unique numbers from 1 to 50. The tool can select five different values, such as 4, 18, 27, 36, and 49. The exact results will change each time.

Why use no-repeat numbers?

  • Pick unique quiz questions.
  • Assign numbered prizes to different winners.
  • Create a random playing order.
  • Select different players for turns.
  • Build random samples from a numbered list.

Important limit

You cannot select more unique numbers than exist in the range. From 1–10, for example, at most 10 unique numbers can be selected.

FAQs

Can random numbers repeat?

They can unless no-repeat selection is used.

How do I pick 10 unique numbers from 1 to 100?

Set the range to 1–100 and request 10 selections with repeats disabled.

Can I generate all numbers in a range in random order?

Yes. That is effectively a shuffled list or random permutation.

Does no-repeat mean the results are predictable?

No. It only removes already selected values from the remaining pool.
