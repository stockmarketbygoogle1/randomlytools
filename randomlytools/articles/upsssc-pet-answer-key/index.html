Official answer key kahan check karein?

  1. UPSSSC ki official website open karein.
  2. Latest notices, answer-key ya candidate-login section mein PET se related notice dekhein.
  3. Notice ki exam year, paper/shift aur publication date verify karein.
  4. Agar login ya response sheet link diya ho, to apne official credentials se hi access karein.
  5. Answer key PDF aur response sheet ko download karke question number, booklet/series aur shift ko dhyan se match karein.

Answer key se marks kaise calculate karein?

Agar applicable official marking scheme har sahi answer par 1 mark aur har galat answer par 0.25 mark deduction batati hai, to raw score ka formula hoga: Correct answers − (Wrong answers × 0.25). Unattempted questions ko tabhi zero count karein jab published instructions aisa kehti hon.

Example ke liye, 70 correct aur 20 wrong answers par raw score = 70 − (20 × 0.25) = 65. Ye sirf formula ka example hai, official result ya cutoff prediction nahi.

Objection ya challenge window

Agar provisional key ke against objection facility announce hoti hai, to deadline, allowed evidence, fee aur submission method official notice se hi verify karein. Har notice mein same process ya dates assume na karein. Apne records ke liye question number, answer-key option aur supporting source note kar sakte hain.

Answer key status kaise samjhein?

Answer key ek hi baar mein final status tak nahi pahunchti. Exam authority provisional key publish kar sakti hai, candidates ko specified period mein objections submit karne ka mauka de sakti hai, aur review ke baad final key/result process kar sakti hai. Har PET cycle mein exact dates aur procedure alag ho sakte hain, isliye neeche ka status tracker general guide hai—live official status ka substitute nahi.

Shift aur booklet series match karna kyun zaroori hai?

Multi-shift exam mein exam date, shift aur question booklet series ko ek saath verify karna important hai. Answer key ka option A kisi doosri series mein same question ke liye different position par ho sakta hai, kyunki question order ya option order badla ho sakta hai. Isliye sirf question number dekh kar response compare karna galat score de sakta hai.

  • Notice par exam year aur publication date check karein.
  • Apni date aur shift ka reference identify karein.
  • Booklet series/code ko response sheet ya exam record se match karein.
  • Question number aur option mapping ko same series ke saath compare karein.
  • Agar document unclear ho, to score estimate ko provisional samjhein aur official clarification ka wait karein.

Objection file karne se pehle kya taiyar karein?

Agar provisional key ke against objection portal khulta hai, to pehle notice ko poora padhein. Deadline, allowed question IDs, evidence format, upload limit, fee (agar applicable ho) aur correction rules official notice se hi lein. Koi bhi past-year fee ya deadline automatically current cycle par apply na karein.

Apne reference ke liye disputed question number, provisional key ka answer, aapka proposed answer aur supporting textbook/official source note kar sakte hain. Sirf social media post, coaching explanation ya unsourced screenshot par depend karne se objection weak ho sakta hai. Ye general preparation advice hai; actual submission ka final process Commission ke portal aur notice par depend karta hai.

Apne estimated marks ko responsibly kaise use karein?

Answer key matching ke baad score estimate useful ho sakta hai, lekin use final scorecard na samjhein. Agar aapne exam ke baad answers memory se likhe hain, to recall mistakes possible hain. Agar key provisional hai, to objections ke baad answers change ho sakte hain. Dropped questions ya bonus marks ka treatment bhi official instructions se determine hoga; har dropped question ko khud se +1 ya zero assign na karein.
