UPSSSC PET Score Validity 2026: Kitne Saal Valid Hai?

PET ek preliminary screening test hai. Score ki validity, shortlist hone ke rules aur kisi specific post ke liye eligibility—ye teen alag cheezein hain. Inhe ek jaisa na samjhein.

Reviewed by RandomlyTools Editorial Team · Last reviewed: 9 October 2026

Short answer: UPSSSC PET 2026 ke liye published notification aur sambandhit Government Order ke mutabik score ki validity result Commission ki website par declare/upload hone ki date se 3 saal hai. Kisi bhi recruitment ke liye apply karne se pehle latest UPSSSC notification mein rule dobara check karein.

1. UPSSSC PET score kitne saal valid rehta hai?

PET score validity ko lekar pehle ke kuch material mein ek saal ka rule mil sakta hai. Baad mein score validity badli gayi. PET 2026 ke notification coverage mein Uttar Pradesh Government Order No. 316/47-Ka-3-2025, dated 28 April 2025, ke tahat score ko result Commission ki website par upload/declare hone ki date se 3 saal tak valid bataya gaya hai. Isliye purane article ya social-media post ko current rule samajhne ke bajay apne exam year ka official notification dekhein.

Validity ki shuruaat exam dene ki date se nahi, balki result upload/declare hone ki date se judi hai. Apne scorecard ki release date aur recruitment advertisement ki application conditions dono note karna useful hai. Agar rule mein future mein amendment aaye, to latest official order purani guide par priority rakhega.

Source check: UPSSSC PET 2025 notification ki available PDF copy mein Government Order No. 316/47-Ka-3-2025, dated 28 April 2025, ke tahat 3-year validity aur multiple valid scores ke case mein highest valid normalised score ka rule diya gaya hai. 2. PET score ka use kahan hota hai?

UPSSSC PET ko Uttar Pradesh mein Commission ke kai Group C recruitment processes ke liye preliminary screening/eligibility stage ke roop mein use kiya jata hai. Relevant recruitment advertisement decide karta hai ki kaun PET score use kar sakta hai, minimum conditions kya hain, aur shortlisted candidates ko agle stage mein kaise bulaya jayega.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki PET score apne aap kisi naukri ki guarantee deta hai. PET score ek recruitment ka hissa hai. Specific post ke liye educational qualification, age limit, category-related rules, application submission, required documents aur baaki selection stages alag se satisfy karne hote hain.

3. PET ke baad Mains ya next stage kaise hota hai?

Jab UPSSSC kisi post ki recruitment advertisement nikalta hai, to us notice mein PET score ke aadhar par shortlisting ka process diya hota hai. Eligible candidates ko us recruitment ke liye alag se application dena pad sakta hai. Uske baad notice ke mutabik written Mains examination, skill test, document verification ya anya stages ho sakte hain. Har vacancy mein stages ek jaise hon, ye zaroori nahi.

  1. Desired post ki official recruitment advertisement padhein.
  2. Check karein ki PET year/score validity aur required score conditions kya hain.
  3. Us recruitment ke liye application window ke andar alag application submit karein, agar notice maangta hai.
  4. Shortlisting aur next-stage instructions ke liye official website aur apne application dashboard ko check karte rahein.
  5. Admit card, exam date, document list aur deadlines ko official notice se hi confirm karein.

4. Agar mere paas ek se zyada valid PET score hain?

PET 2026 notification ke coverage ke mutabik, agar candidate ke paas ek se zyada PET scores validity period ke andar valid hain, to specific Main Examination ke liye highest valid normalised score ke basis par apply/shortlisting ka provision ho sakta hai. Is rule ka exact application relevant recruitment notice aur Commission ke latest instructions par depend karega. Sirf raw score compare karke final decision na lein—scorecard par diya gaya score type aur advertisement ki language dekhein.

5. PET scorecard kaise download karein?

  1. UPSSSC ki official website kholein.
  2. Results/notice section mein apne PET year ka result ya scorecard link dhoondhein.
  3. Portal par maange gaye details ko sirf official login page par enter karein. Required details exam year ke hisaab se alag ho sakti hain.
  4. Scorecard khulne par naam aur doosri details, exam status aur score fields ko dhyan se verify karein.
  5. PDF save karein aur personal records ke liye ek backup rakhein. Public groups mein personal scorecard ya registration details post na karein.

Agar scorecard link nahi milta, to random third-party download links par personal details enter na karein. Official notice ka wait karein ya Commission ke published contact/help instructions follow karein.

6. Scorecard mein kaun se scores ho sakte hain?

Scorecard format mein absolute/raw score, normalised score aur percentile jaise labels ho sakte hain. Raw score correct aur wrong answers ke marking rules se nikalta hai. Normalised score multi-shift evaluation ke liye Commission ke published method se related hota hai, aur percentile relative performance ko represent karta hai. Ye terms interchangeable nahi hain.

PET Marks Calculator sirf entered correct/wrong counts se raw-score estimate deta hai. Ye official scorecard retrieve nahi karta aur normalized score ya percentile predict nahi karta. Agar aap apne estimated raw marks check karna chahte hain, calculator mein correct aur wrong answers enter karein.

7. Validity aur cutoff mein kya farq hai?

Validity ka matlab score ko specified time period ke andar use karne ka rule hai. Cutoff ya shortlist threshold ek particular recruitment, vacancy, category, candidate pool aur official process se sambandhit ho sakta hai. Teen saal valid score ka matlab ye nahi ki har post ke liye aap automatically shortlist ho jayenge.

Isliye kisi bhi post ka “safe score” ya cutoff tab tak final na maanein jab tak us recruitment ke official notice/result se confirm na ho. Purani bharti ka cutoff agle recruitment mein same rahe, iski guarantee nahi hoti.

8. Candidates ki aam galtiyan

  • Purane one-year validity rule ko current PET 2026 rule samajhna.
  • Score validity ko job selection ki guarantee samajhna.
  • Har Group C post ke liye same eligibility ya cutoff assume karna.
  • Raw score ko normalised score/percentile samajhna.
  • Specific recruitment ka advertisement padhe bina sirf PET score ke basis par eligibility maan lena.
  • Scorecard download karne ke liye unofficial site par sensitive login details dena.

9. Quick checklist

  • Apne PET year aur scorecard ki upload/result date note karein.
  • Validity ke liye latest official order aur PET notification check karein.
  • Target recruitment ki age, qualification aur PET score conditions padhein.
  • Application window aur next-stage dates ko official sources se verify karein.
  • Scorecard ko securely save karein aur personal data public na karein.

Frequently asked questions

Kya UPSSSC PET score 3 saal valid hai?

PET 2026 ke published notification coverage ke mutabik validity result declare/upload hone ki date se 3 saal hai. Final confirmation ke liye latest official notification/order dekhein.

Kya PET score se seedhi naukri milti hai?

Nahi. PET preliminary screening ka hissa hai. Har recruitment ke alag eligibility aur selection stages hote hain.

Kya ek PET score se kai recruitments ke liye apply kar sakte hain?

Jahan relevant recruitment notice valid PET score ko accept karta hai, wahan uske rules ke mutabik apply kiya ja sakta hai. Har vacancy ki conditions alag se check karein.

Kya ye calculator official scorecard dikhata hai?

Nahi. Ye independent tool raw-score estimate deta hai. Official scorecard ke liye UPSSSC website use karein.

Agar mere do PET scores valid hain to kaunsa use hoga?

Multiple valid scores ke case mein highest valid normalised score ka provision ho sakta hai; exact rule relevant notice se verify karein.
