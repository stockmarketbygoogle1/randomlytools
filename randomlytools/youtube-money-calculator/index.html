How Much Money Can You Make on YouTube?

Your earnings depend more on the location and type of viewers than simply the number of subscribers. This calculator estimates revenue using monthly views and a planning RPM, then shows daily, monthly and yearly amounts.

How YouTube Earnings Are Calculated

In RPM mode, the core calculation is monthly views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. RPM is useful because it represents creator revenue per 1,000 views. If you know your actual RPM from YouTube Studio, enter it in Advanced mode for a more useful estimate.

YouTube RPM vs CPM

CPM is an advertiser-side metric, while RPM is a creator-side revenue metric. They should not be mixed into one formula. The calculator therefore uses RPM directly for its main estimate and treats monetized-view/CPM assumptions as an advanced planning concept.

Audience country can strongly affect advertising value. The country selector uses conservative planning bands rather than claiming a universal official RPM. Your own YouTube Studio RPM should take priority.

How Many Views Do You Need to Reach an Income Goal?

Required views are calculated as income goal ÷ RPM × 1,000. Use your actual RPM whenever possible. A finance channel and an entertainment channel with the same number of views can produce very different revenue.

FAQ

Is this an exact YouTube earnings calculator?

No. It is an estimate. Actual earnings can change because RPM changes over time and differs by channel and audience.

Should I use CPM or RPM?

Use RPM when you want to estimate creator earnings. CPM is mainly useful for understanding advertiser-side pricing.

Does audience country matter?

Yes. Advertiser demand and viewer market can cause large differences in RPM.
