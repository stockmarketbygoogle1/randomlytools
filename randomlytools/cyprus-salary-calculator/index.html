Cyprus Salary Calculator: Gross to Net Pay

If you are working in Cyprus, comparing a job offer, relocating, or checking your expected monthly paycheck, gross salary is only the starting point. Your take-home pay can be affected by income tax, employee Social Insurance and the General Healthcare System (GHS) contribution. This Cyprus salary calculator combines those core deductions into a simple 2026 estimate.

The calculator is designed around the Cyprus 2026 tax reform. It uses the new €22,000 tax-free band and the progressive rates published by the Cyprus Tax Department. It also uses the 2026 Social Insurance ceiling and the employee GHS rate. The result is an estimate, not an official payroll calculation.

How to Use the Cyprus Net Salary Calculator

  1. Enter your gross monthly salary: use the amount before employee deductions.
  2. Add other annual taxable income: optional; leave at zero if your salary is your only income.
  3. Add other annual deductions: optional; use only deductions you are confident apply to you.
  4. Calculate: the tool estimates annual income tax, Social Insurance, GHS and your monthly take-home pay.

Cyprus Income Tax Rates 2026

From tax year 2026, Cyprus increased the tax-free threshold from €19,500 to €22,000. The new progressive income-tax bands are:

Annual taxable incomeTax rate
€0 – €22,0000%
€22,001 – €32,00020%
€32,001 – €42,00025%
€42,001 – €72,00030%
Above €72,00035%

The bands are progressive. For example, a salary that crosses €42,000 does not have its entire income taxed at 30%; only the portion inside that bracket is taxed at 30%.

Cyprus Social Insurance 2026

For employees, the Social Insurance contribution rate is 8.8%. The official 2026 maximum monthly insurable earnings limit is €5,742. This means the calculator caps the employee Social Insurance base at €5,742 per month for the core estimate.

The official Social Insurance information confirms that, from 2024 onward, employees contribute 8.8% while the employer also contributes 8.8% and the Republic contributes 5.2% to the Social Insurance Fund.

Cyprus GHS Contribution 2026

The employee GHS contribution is 2.65%. GHS contributions are subject to the applicable €180,000 annual ceiling. The calculator therefore caps the GHS base at €180,000 per year for this basic employee estimate.

Example: €3,000 Gross Monthly Salary

With a gross monthly salary of €3,000, annual gross salary is €36,000. Before any other personal deductions or reliefs, the calculator estimates employee Social Insurance using the 8.8% rate, GHS at 2.65%, and income tax using the 2026 progressive bands.

Because the first €22,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0%, a €36,000 salary does not pay 20% on the entire amount. Only the taxable portion above €22,000 enters the 20% bracket, subject to applicable deductions and allowances.

Cyprus Salary After Tax: What Can Change the Result?

A basic gross-to-net calculator cannot capture every individual tax situation. Your actual payslip can differ because Cyprus tax rules allow certain deductions, allowances and reliefs. Examples can include approved pension or provident-fund contributions, medical or insurance-related deductions, family or child-related reliefs, and specific employment-related reliefs.

The 2026 tax reform also changed several personal tax rules. If you are a new resident, a high-income employee, a parent, or someone with significant deductions, use the calculator as a starting estimate and check the official rules for your circumstances.

Cyprus Net to Gross Salary

If you know the net salary you want rather than the gross salary, the calculation needs to be solved in reverse because Cyprus income tax is progressive and Social Insurance has a ceiling. The current version of this tool focuses on gross-to-net calculations so that the displayed result remains transparent and easy to verify. A future net-to-gross mode can be added without changing the core 2026 tax model.

2026 Cyprus Tax Reform

The Cyprus Tax Department says the 2026 reform applies from the 2026 tax year and raises the tax-free income level to €22,000. The official reform material also provides an income-tax calculation tool and explanatory guides. This page uses those official published rates as its base data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Cyprus salary calculator for 2026 based on?

It uses the official 2026 income-tax bands, the 8.8% employee Social Insurance rate with the 2026 €5,742 monthly insurable-earnings ceiling, and the 2.65% employee GHS contribution subject to the €180,000 annual ceiling.

What salary is tax-free in Cyprus in 2026?

The first €22,000 of annual taxable income is taxed at 0% under the 2026 income-tax scale. Social Insurance and GHS contributions can still apply to employment income.

Is Cyprus salary tax calculated on gross salary?

Income tax is calculated on taxable or chargeable income after applicable deductions and allowances. This calculator provides a simplified estimate and allows an optional other-deductions input.

Does Cyprus have a 13th salary?

A 13th salary can form part of employment income when paid under the employment arrangement. If you receive one, include the additional gross amount in your annual salary when using an annualized estimate.

Is this an official Cyprus payroll calculator?

No. RandomlyTools is an independent calculator. Use official Cyprus Tax Department and Social Insurance guidance for filing, payroll or tax-advice decisions.
