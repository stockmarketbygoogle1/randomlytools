What Can I Make With the Ingredients I Have?

This recipe finder helps you turn common pantry and refrigerator ingredients into practical meal ideas. Select ingredients such as chicken, eggs, rice, potatoes, tomatoes, onions, pasta, cheese and vegetables, then compare recipes by how closely they match what you already have.

Recipe Finder by Ingredients

Instead of searching for a recipe first and checking the shopping list afterward, start with the food you already have. The tool ranks its built-in recipe ideas by ingredient matches and shows any additional ingredients that may be needed.

How to Use This Recipe Finder

  1. Choose ingredients from the quick ingredient list or type your own.
  2. Select a meal type if you want breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack ideas.
  3. Choose vegetarian or vegan when needed.
  4. Click Find Recipes to see matching ideas.
  5. Check the missing-ingredient list before cooking.

Recipes From Ingredients You Have

Good recipe discovery starts with realistic ingredients. The tool includes simple dishes such as fried rice, vegetable pasta, omelets, pancakes, chickpea salad, tomato pasta, chicken rice bowls and other everyday meals. Results are suggestions, so always check quantities, substitutions and food-safety requirements before cooking.

Features

  • Search recipe ideas by ingredients you already have.
  • Choose breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack.
  • Filter vegetarian and vegan ideas.
  • See an ingredient-match percentage.
  • See which ingredients are missing.
  • Runs in your browser without an account.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a recipe finder by ingredients?

It is a tool that compares the ingredients you have with recipe ingredient lists and shows the closest matching meal ideas.

What can I make with the ingredients I have?

Add the ingredients you have, then use Find Recipes. The results are ranked by how many required ingredients match.

Can I find dinner recipes from ingredients?

Yes. Select Dinner in the meal filter to focus on dinner recipe ideas.

Can I find vegetarian recipes?

Yes. Choose Vegetarian or Vegan in the diet filter before finding recipes.

Does this recipe generator use an external API?

No. The current tool uses a curated set of recipe ideas stored in the page and performs matching in your browser.
