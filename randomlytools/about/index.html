About RandomlyTools

Free, fast, and privacy-focused online generators, calculators, and practical tools for everyday use.

Hi, I’m Aayush Ahake, the owner of RandomlyTools. I created this website to make small everyday tasks a little easier. Sometimes we just need a random name, a quick calculation, a username idea, or an answer to a simple question. My aim is to provide tools that can do those jobs without making things complicated.

RandomlyTools started with random generators and has slowly grown into a larger collection of free online tools. Today, the website includes random name and username generators, calculators, YouTube earnings tools, salary calculators, cricket calculators, and other practical utilities.

I try to keep the tools simple, fast, and easy to understand. Whenever a calculation involves a formula, estimate, assumption, or limitation, I try to explain it clearly so you can understand where the result comes from.

What Does RandomlyTools Offer?

There are several types of tools available on RandomlyTools:

  • Random generators: names, numbers, usernames, team names, business names, restaurant names, pet names, and other creative ideas.
  • Calculators: attendance, advertising revenue, salary, fence post depth, recipe-related calculations, and other practical calculations.
  • YouTube tools: YouTube money, RPM, Shorts earnings, and country-specific YouTube earnings calculators.
  • Cricket tools: net run rate, required run rate, chase, and qualification calculators.
  • Practical utilities: small browser-based tools made to help with specific everyday questions and tasks.

Most tools are made to work directly in your browser, and many do not require an account. You can visit the RandomlyTools homepage to see the complete collection.

Why I Built RandomlyTools

I wanted to build a website where people could get a useful result quickly instead of going through a lot of unnecessary information. A simple tool should feel simple to use.

At the same time, I don't want the tools to feel like a black box. Where it makes sense, I include formulas, examples, explanations, and limitations so users can understand the result instead of only seeing a number on the screen.

I also want RandomlyTools to remain fast, accessible, privacy-focused, and useful as the website grows.

How I Create the Content and Tools

RandomlyTools combines interactive tools with helpful explanatory content. Depending on the topic, a page may include a calculator or generator along with formulas, examples, FAQs, related tools, and practical information.

For topics that can change over time, such as YouTube earnings or salary calculations, I try to explain the important factors behind an estimate. Things like country, niche, views, rates, taxes, and other assumptions can affect the final result, so estimates should be treated as estimates unless a tool specifically says otherwise.

Independent Website

RandomlyTools is an independent online tools website. It is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Instagram, Discord, or any other third-party platform mentioned by individual tools unless that relationship is explicitly stated on the relevant page.

The tools are available for personal use and can also be useful for creators, students, businesses, sports fans, and anyone who needs a quick calculation, random result, or idea.

Accuracy and Updates

I try to keep the formulas, explanations, and results as accurate and useful as possible. Some calculators provide estimates because real-world results can vary from person to person or situation to situation.

I review and improve the tools and content when needed, especially when a calculation depends on changing rates, platform policies, taxes, or other information that can change over time.

Privacy and Simplicity

Where possible, RandomlyTools is designed to do the work directly in the browser. Many tools do not require an account, and the goal is to collect as little unnecessary information as possible while keeping the tools useful.
