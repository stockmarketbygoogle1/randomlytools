About RandomlyTools

Free, fast, and privacy-focused online generators, calculators, and practical tools for everyday use.

Welcome to RandomlyTools. We build simple browser-based tools that help people solve common everyday tasks quickly without unnecessary complexity.

RandomlyTools started with random generators and has grown into a broader collection of free online utilities. The website now includes random name and username generators, calculators, YouTube earnings tools, salary calculators, cricket calculators, and other practical tools.

Our goal is to keep these tools easy to understand, fast to use, and useful across different types of everyday questions and decisions.

What Does RandomlyTools Offer?

RandomlyTools provides several categories of browser-based tools:

  • Random generators: names, numbers, usernames, team names, business names, restaurant names, pet names, and other creative ideas.
  • Calculators: attendance, advertising revenue, salary, fence post depth, recipe-related calculations, and other practical calculations.
  • YouTube tools: YouTube money, RPM, Shorts earnings, and country-specific YouTube earnings calculators.
  • Cricket tools: net run rate, required run rate, chase, and qualification calculators.
  • Practical utilities: useful tools designed to answer specific everyday questions directly in the browser.

Most tools are designed to work directly in the browser and many do not require an account. Visit the RandomlyTools homepage to explore the complete collection.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create useful, original, and easy-to-understand tools instead of making visitors search through unnecessary information to get a simple answer.

We aim to explain important formulas, assumptions, examples, and limitations where they matter so users can understand how a result is produced rather than simply seeing a number.

We also work to keep the website fast, accessible, privacy-focused, and regularly improved as new tools and useful topics are added.

Our Content Approach

RandomlyTools combines interactive tools with explanatory content. Tool pages may include formulas, examples, FAQs, related tools, and practical guidance so that the information remains useful even before or alongside using the calculator or generator.

For topics that can change over time, such as YouTube earnings or salary calculations, we explain that estimates depend on factors such as country, niche, views, rates, taxes, and other assumptions. Results should therefore be treated as estimates unless a tool specifically states otherwise.

About the Website

RandomlyTools is an independent online tools website focused on practical browser-based utilities. We are not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Instagram, Discord, or any other third-party platform mentioned by individual tools unless explicitly stated on the relevant page.

The website includes free tools for personal use as well as tools that can help creators, students, businesses, sports fans, and anyone who needs a quick calculation or idea generator.

Accuracy and Updates

We try to provide accurate formulas, clear explanations, and useful results. Some calculators provide estimates because real-world results can vary. We review and improve tools and content as needed, especially when a calculation depends on changing rates, platform policies, or other external conditions.
