What You Can Do With RandomlyTools

RandomlyTools is a collection of focused online utilities rather than a single-purpose calculator. The homepage groups the tools by the type of task they help with, so you can move from a simple random choice to a more specific calculator without needing an account.

Generate Names, Usernames and Ideas

Use the name and username generators when you need starting ideas for people, characters, pets, Discord profiles or Instagram handles. Business-focused generators cover shops, restaurants, coffee shops, companies and brands, giving you a quick list of ideas to review and refine.

Make Random Choices and Organize Groups

The Random Number Picker can select values from a custom range, while the Random Team Generator can split participants into groups. The Random Country Generator is useful for quizzes, geography practice, games and other activities where you need a country chosen at random.

Use Calculators for Planning and Estimates

RandomlyTools also includes attendance, advertising, salary and YouTube-related calculators. These tools are intended for planning and estimation. Results can depend on the inputs, assumptions, country, audience or other conditions used by the individual calculator, so important financial or salary decisions should be checked against current official information.

YouTube and Creator Tools

The YouTube section includes tools for estimated earnings, RPM and Shorts revenue, including country-focused calculators for India and Iceland. They are useful for exploring scenarios and understanding how views, RPM and audience factors can affect an estimate; they are not guarantees of actual creator revenue.

Gaming and Everyday Utility

For gamers, the GTA 6 Download Time Calculator estimates download duration from an assumed game size and internet speed. The Recipe Finder by Ingredients helps turn ingredients already available at home into recipe ideas. These tools are designed for quick, practical use rather than replacing official product information or professional advice.

How to Use the Tools

  1. Choose a category or search: Use the category buttons or search box to find the tool that matches your task.
  2. Open the tool: Each card takes you to a dedicated page with its own inputs and instructions.
  3. Enter your information: Follow the fields and options shown by that specific tool.
  4. Review the result: Random generators return generated results, while calculators provide results based on the inputs and assumptions used.
