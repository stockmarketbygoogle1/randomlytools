How Much Do YouTube Shorts Pay?

Shorts revenue is generally much lower per 1,000 views than many long-form channels because Shorts monetization works differently. Your actual result depends on eligible views, audience mix, advertising demand and YouTube's revenue-sharing system.

Shorts Earnings Formula

For a simple planning estimate, earnings = views ÷ 1,000 × Shorts RPM. Use your own Studio-derived RPM when available.
