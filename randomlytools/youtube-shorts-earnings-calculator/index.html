How Much Do YouTube Shorts Pay?

YouTube Shorts earnings are different from traditional long-form video earnings. Shorts monetization depends on the Shorts revenue-sharing system, eligible views, audience mix and other monetization factors.

Because Shorts RPM can be very small, a channel may need hundreds of thousands or millions of views to generate meaningful monthly revenue. This calculator is for planning, not a guaranteed payout.

YouTube Shorts Earnings Formula

For a simple RPM-based estimate, earnings = views ÷ 1,000 × Shorts RPM. If your Shorts RPM is $0.10, 1 million views gives a mathematical estimate of $100.

Why Shorts RPM Is Different

Long-form videos and Shorts have different monetization mechanics. A long-form RPM should not automatically be used to forecast Shorts income. Audience geography, eligible views, content and advertiser demand can also change results.

Does Audience Country Affect Shorts Earnings?

Yes. Viewer location can influence advertising value and the revenue available to creators. A channel with mostly Indian viewers can have a different effective RPM from one with a mostly US, UK or other international audience.

How Many Shorts Views Do You Need to Make $100 or $1,000?

At a $0.10 RPM, about 1 million views would be needed for $100 and 10 million views for $1,000. At a $0.20 RPM, those requirements would be roughly half.

Shorts vs Long-form Earnings

Shorts can generate large reach, but revenue per 1,000 views is often much lower than long-form. For long-form planning, use the YouTube Money Calculator.
