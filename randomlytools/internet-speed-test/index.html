What Is an Internet Speed Test?

An internet speed test measures how quickly data can move between your device and a test server. The main measurements are download speed, upload speed and latency. Download and upload bandwidth are normally shown in megabits per second (Mbps), while latency is shown in milliseconds (ms).

How to Check Internet Speed

  1. Connect your device to the internet you want to test.
  2. Close large downloads, uploads and bandwidth-heavy apps where practical.
  3. Click Start Speed Test.
  4. Wait for the measurements to complete.
  5. Review your download speed, upload speed and latency.

Download Speed vs Upload Speed

Download speed describes data transferred from the test server to your device. It matters for activities such as loading websites, streaming video and downloading files.

Upload speed describes data transferred from your device to the test server. It matters for activities such as sending large files, video calls, live streaming and cloud backups.

What Is Ping or Latency?

Latency is the time taken for a network request to travel and receive a response. A lower latency usually means a quicker response from the network. Latency can be especially noticeable in interactive activities such as online gaming and video calls.

Why Is My Internet Speed Different From My Plan?

A speed-test result can differ from the advertised or subscribed speed because of Wi-Fi signal quality, router performance, network congestion, device limitations, background traffic, server distance and other conditions. A single test is a measurement at one point in time, not a permanent specification of your connection.

Wi-Fi Speed Test Tips

  • Test from the same location where you normally use the connection.
  • For comparison, repeat the test at different times of day.
  • Compare Wi-Fi with a wired Ethernet connection when available.
  • Keep other high-bandwidth devices as idle as practical during the test.
  • Restarting network equipment can change conditions, but it should not be necessary before every measurement.

Internet Speed Test vs Broadband Speed Test

These terms generally describe the same basic type of measurement: checking the performance of an internet connection. The connection may be fiber, cable, DSL, fixed wireless, mobile broadband or another access technology.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my internet speed?

Click Start Speed Test above and wait for the download, upload and latency measurements to finish.

What is a good internet speed?

There is no single speed that is ideal for everyone. The amount of bandwidth you need depends on the number of users, devices and activities on the connection.

What does Mbps mean?

Mbps means megabits per second. It is a common unit used to describe internet download and upload bandwidth.

Why does my Wi-Fi speed change?

Wi-Fi performance can change because of signal strength, interference, distance from the router, network congestion and other devices using the connection.

Why are speed-test results different each time?

Internet conditions change over time. Background traffic, congestion, Wi-Fi conditions, device load and test-server conditions can all affect a measurement.
