What Is a CSC Photocopy Cost Calculator?

A CSC photocopy cost calculator helps a VLE, cyber cafe or photocopy shop estimate the actual cost of producing a photocopied page. Instead of assuming one fixed rate, this calculator lets you enter your paper cost, copier or toner cost, expected page yield, electricity, maintenance and other expenses.

How to Calculate Photocopy Cost Per Page

The basic idea is to add the cost of each component used to produce one photocopied page:

Print cost per page = paper cost + copier/toner cost per page + electricity cost per page + maintenance/waste + other per-page costs

Printer or toner cost per page can be estimated as:

Printer/toner cost per page = copier or toner supply cost ÷ expected page yield

For example, if a ₹3,000 supply is expected to produce 1,500 pages, its supply cost is ₹2 per page. The actual yield depends on the copier, cartridge, toner, coverage and usage, so use the yield that best matches your own setup.

Why Use Your Own Photocopy Costs?

Printing costs can vary widely between service counters because paper prices, copier models, consumables, electricity costs, maintenance and wastage are different. A local photocopy shop may also charge different amounts for black-and-white, colour, single-side and double-side photocopying.

Using your own numbers makes the result useful for pricing decisions without presenting a changing service rate as an official rule.

Photocopy Cost Example

Suppose your paper costs ₹0.50 per sheet, your copier/toner supply costs ₹3,000 and gives an expected 1,500 pages, electricity is ₹0.05 per page, and maintenance/waste is ₹0.10 per page. The estimated cost is ₹2.65 per page before any other costs you add. If you charge ₹5 per page, the estimated gross profit is ₹2.35 per page before other business expenses.

What Can Change Your Photocopy Cost?

  • Paper: paper size, GSM, brand and local purchase price can change the cost per sheet.
  • Ink or toner: actual yield can vary with page coverage and print settings.
  • Electricity: power consumption and local electricity cost affect operating cost.
  • Maintenance: cleaning, servicing, replacement parts and failed prints add costs.
  • Print type: colour and black-and-white photocopying can have very different consumable costs.
  • Duplex photocopying: double-sided jobs change how many printed sides you produce per sheet.

CSC Photocopy Cost Calculator vs Official Service Rates

This tool is intentionally different from a rate list. It calculates your own operating cost and pricing using your inputs. It does not claim that a particular amount is an official CSC commission or government-mandated print charge.
