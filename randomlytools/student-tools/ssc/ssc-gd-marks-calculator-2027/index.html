SSC GD Marking Scheme

ItemCurrent CBT patternEffect on score
Total Questions8020 questions in each of 4 sections
Maximum Marks1602 marks per correct answer
Correct Answer+2Adds 2 marks
Wrong Answer-0.25Deducts 0.25 mark
Unattempted0No marks added or deducted
Formula:
(Correct Answers × 2) − (Wrong Answers × 0.25)

How SSC GD Marks Are Calculated

Unattempted questions do not reduce the raw score. The calculator does not treat a blank field as zero; required answer inputs must be entered.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is SSC GD score calculated?

Use correct answers × 2 minus wrong answers × 0.25 for the raw CBT score under the current pattern.

How many marks is the SSC GD CBT?

The current pattern uses 80 questions for a maximum of 160 marks.

Is there negative marking in SSC GD?

Yes. A wrong answer carries a 0.25 mark deduction under the current pattern.

Does the calculator show final normalized marks?

No. It calculates the raw score. Official SSC results should be used for normalized marks.

Can SSC GD cutoff vary by state and category?

Yes. Official GD result lists can have different selection and cutoff conditions. Check the exact official result for your cycle.
