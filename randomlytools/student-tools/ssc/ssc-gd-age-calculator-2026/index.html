How to use the SSC GD calculator

  1. Enter your DOB from your matriculation/secondary certificate.
  2. Keep the calculation date at 1 January 2026.
  3. Click Check Age Eligibility.
  4. Read the exact age and standard-range result.

Age relaxation

Eligible categories and special groups can receive relaxation under applicable recruitment rules. This tool checks the standard 18–23 range only.

Use the result correctly

Age is only one part of recruitment eligibility. Other educational, nationality, physical, medical and recruitment conditions may also apply.
