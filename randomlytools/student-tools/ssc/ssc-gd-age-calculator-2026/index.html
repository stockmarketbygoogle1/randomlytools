How to Use the SSC GD Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded in your prescribed DOB proof.
  2. Keep the calculation date at 1 January 2026 for the SSC GD 2026 check.
  3. Click Check Age Eligibility.
  4. Read your exact age and the standard 18–23 result.

Which Date of Birth Should You Enter?

Use the DOB recorded in the document accepted by the recruitment process. A small difference of one day can change a boundary-date result, so do not estimate your DOB from age or school records that are not the prescribed proof.

Why the SSC GD Crucial Date Matters

For this recruitment, age is reckoned with reference to 1 January 2026. That is why the calculator uses that date instead of today's date. Changing the calculation date changes the exact age and can also change whether a candidate falls inside the standard DOB window.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC GD age limit in 2026?

The standard age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 1 January 2026.

What is the SSC GD 2026 DOB range?

For the standard age limit, the DOB window is 2 January 2003 to 1 January 2008.

Does the calculator include age relaxation?

No. It checks the standard 18–23 range. Applicable relaxation for eligible categories or special groups should be verified against the official notice.

Can a boundary-date DOB be eligible?

Yes, boundary dates can be eligible when they fall within the prescribed DOB window. Use the table above to understand the two ends of the standard range.

Why does my age today differ from the calculator result?

Because this calculator is designed for the SSC GD 2026 age-reckoning date of 1 January 2026, not necessarily today's date.
