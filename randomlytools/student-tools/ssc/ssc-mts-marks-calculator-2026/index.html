SSC MTS Marks Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

How is SSC MTS marks calculated?

Session-I raw marks are correct answers × 3 because there is no negative marking. Session-II raw marks are (correct × 3) − wrong.

How many marks are there in SSC MTS Session-I?

Session-I has 40 questions for a maximum of 120 marks under the published exam structure.

Is there negative marking in SSC MTS Session-I?

No. The published SSC MTS scheme states that there is no negative marking in Session-I.

What is the negative marking in SSC MTS Session-II?

One mark is deducted for each wrong answer in Session-II under the published scheme.

What are the SSC MTS Session-I qualifying marks?

The qualifying percentages are category-based. Use the table on this page for the standard qualifying thresholds and verify the latest notification.

Is SSC MTS raw score the same as normalized score?

No. Raw marks are calculated from your answers. SSC can normalize scores when the examination is conducted in multiple shifts.

Can I calculate SSC MTS marks using the answer key?

Yes. Count correct and wrong responses from the response sheet/answer key and enter them in the calculator.

Does SSC MTS cutoff vary by state?

Yes. Official MTS final-result tables can show separate cutoffs by State/UT, age group and category.

Does qualifying Session-I mean I am selected?

No. Session-I is qualifying. Final MTS merit is based on Session-II normalized performance and the applicable vacancy, preference and allocation rules.

Does this calculator predict final selection?

No. It calculates raw marks and provides reference information. Only SSC's official result and allocation process determine final selection.
