How the SSC Cutoff Calculator Works

  1. Select your SSC exam. The calculator changes its stages and marking pattern according to the selected examination.
  2. Choose the stage. Some SSC exams have more than one paper or session, and their marks are not interchangeable.
  3. Calculate your score. If the marking scheme is supported, enter correct and wrong answers. The calculator applies the displayed marks and penalty.
  4. Select your category. Category is needed for most cutoff comparisons.
  5. Compare with a cutoff. Use a built-in historical/reference cutoff where available or enter the exact cutoff from the relevant SSC result.

Why There Is No Single SSC Cutoff

SSC cutoff is not one universal number. The Commission can publish separate cutoffs by exam stage, list, post group, category, gender, state/UT, force, area or other recruitment condition. For example, SSC CGL 2025 had separate Tier-I lists for JSO, Statistical Investigator Grade-II and other posts, while SSC CHSL 2025 published separate Tier-I cutoffs for LDC/JSA and different DEO groups.

SSC CGL Cutoff Calculator

For CGL, first calculate your Tier-I raw marks if you have correct and wrong attempts. When comparing with a published Tier-I cutoff, remember that SSC publishes normalized marks for a multi-shift exam. The 2025 revised CGL Tier-I cutoff for the other-post list was reported as 136.40215 for UR, 130.36617 for OBC, 127.41630 for EWS, 115.02843 for SC and 106.38576 for ST. JSO and Statistical Investigator had separate lists and higher category-specific cutoffs.

SSC CHSL Cutoff Calculator

CHSL Tier-I can be compared using the relevant post group. The historical database includes the 2024 LDC/JSA cutoffs as well as the 2025 LDC/JSA/Clerk-Cum-Typist reference. DEO groups have separate cutoffs, so the calculator should not treat all CHSL posts as one cutoff.

SSC GD Cutoff Calculator

GD needs extra care because cutoff comparisons can depend on force, candidate group, state/UT, area and category. A single all-India GD cutoff is therefore not suitable for every candidate. Use the manual cutoff field when the exact state/force/area cutoff from the result is required. Recent published 2025 data shows separate NCB and SSF category cutoffs.

SSC CPO Cutoff Calculator

CPO Paper-I cutoff can differ between female, male and departmental lists. For the 2025 Paper-I result, the reported UR cutoff was 128.01610 for the female list and 119.47517 for the male list, with other category values also differing. Select the correct candidate group before comparing.

SSC Stenographer Cutoff Calculator

Stenographer Grade C and Grade D have separate CBT cutoff lists. In the 2025 CBT result, the UR cutoff was 142.42577 for Group C and 132.30789 for Group D. The calculator therefore asks you to choose the relevant stage/group before a reference comparison.

SSC MTS, JE, JHT & Selection Post Cutoff Calculator

These exams can also be calculated on the same page, but the comparison must respect their exam-specific rules. JE cutoffs, for example, can be based on normalized Paper-I marks, while Selection Post cutoffs can vary by individual post and qualification level. MTS has separate sessions and recruitment rules. When a built-in reference is not appropriate, manual cutoff mode is intentionally provided instead of guessing a universal number.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC Cut Off Calculator?

It calculates supported SSC exam marks and compares them with a selected cutoff reference or a cutoff entered by you.

Which SSC exams are covered?

The calculator covers CGL, CHSL, GD Constable, CPO, MTS, JE, Stenographer, JHT and Selection Post. The architecture can be extended when SSC introduces or changes an examination.

Can I use it for 2026?

Yes. The page is built for the 2026 preparation cycle, but a 2026 official cutoff should only be shown after SSC releases it. Until then, use a previous-cycle reference or manual cutoff.

Why does the calculator sometimes ask for a list or group?

Some SSC exams publish separate cutoffs for different post groups or candidate lists. Using the wrong list can make a comparison misleading.

Can I enter my own cutoff?

Yes. Manual cutoff mode is included so you can paste the exact cutoff from an SSC result or notice for your post, category, state or candidate group.

Is a score above cutoff a selection guarantee?

No. Clearing a stage cutoff only addresses that particular comparison. Final selection can depend on later stages, merit, vacancies, preferences and other recruitment conditions.
