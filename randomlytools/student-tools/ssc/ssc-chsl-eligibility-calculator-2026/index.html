SSC CHSL Educational Qualification 2026

For CHSL 2026, the essential educational qualification is reckoned as on 07 October 2026. A candidate who appeared in Class 12 may apply, but the required result must be declared by the cut-off date.

LDC / JSA12th Standard or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University.
DEO / DEO Grade A — general condition12th Standard or equivalent, subject to the exact post/department requirement.
Specified DEO posts12th Standard in Science stream with Mathematics as a subject.

SSC CHSL Nationality / Citizenship

Age and qualification are only part of eligibility. The SSC notice also contains nationality and citizenship conditions. This calculator does not determine nationality eligibility, so candidates in special nationality categories should verify the exact requirement in the official notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What qualification is required for SSC CHSL 2026?

For LDC/JSA and most DEO posts, candidates need 12th Standard or an equivalent examination. Certain specified DEO posts require Science stream with Mathematics.

What is the SSC CHSL 2026 age limit?

The standard age range is 18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026, subject to applicable relaxation.

What is the SSC CHSL 2026 DOB range?

For the standard 18–27 range, the DOB window is 2 August 1999 to 1 August 2008.

Does this calculator apply age relaxation?

No. It performs the standard age check. Applicable category-specific and special-group relaxation must be verified separately.

Can an awaiting Class 12 result candidate apply?

Yes, a candidate who has appeared can apply provisionally, but the required Class 12 result must be declared and the essential qualification must be possessed on or before 07 October 2026.

What is the SSC CHSL 2026 qualification cut-off date?

The essential educational qualification is reckoned as on 07 October 2026.

Which DEO posts require Science with Mathematics?

Specified DEO / DEO Grade A posts in the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, SSC and Ministry of Culture require 12th Science with Mathematics. Other DEO posts generally follow the standard 12th qualification requirement.
