SSC CHSL Eligibility 2026: quick check

The CHSL eligibility check combines the standard age requirement with educational qualification. For the standard 2026 age check, the crucial date is 1 August 2026 and the age range is 18–27 years.

SSC CHSL educational qualification

Post/conditionBasic qualification
LDC/JSA and most DEO posts12th Standard or equivalent examination
Certain DEO / DEO Grade A posts12th Standard in Science stream with Mathematics as a subject

SSC CHSL age and DOB range

For the standard 18–27 range, the DOB window is 2 August 1999 through 1 August 2008, inclusive. Applicable age relaxation can change the upper-age limit.

How this calculator works

  1. Enter your DOB.
  2. Confirm that you have passed 12th Standard or equivalent.
  3. Select the post condition.
  4. For the special DEO condition, confirm Science with Mathematics.
  5. Read the result and limitation note.

What this tool does not check

This is a basic screening tool. It does not decide nationality, category-specific age relaxation, document validity, every post-specific condition or other requirements in the current SSC notice.
