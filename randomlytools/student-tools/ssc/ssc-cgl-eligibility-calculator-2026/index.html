Frequently Asked Questions

What is the basic educational qualification for SSC CGL 2026?

For all other CGL posts, a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent is required. Some posts have additional qualifications.

What qualification is required for JSO?

JSO requires a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, or a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level.

What qualification is required for Statistical Investigator Grade-II?

The 2026 notice specifies a bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science or Artificial Intelligence.

Can final-year graduation students apply?

Yes, candidates who have appeared in their final year can apply, but they must possess the essential qualification as on 1 August 2026.
