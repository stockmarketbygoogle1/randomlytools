SSC CGL 2026 basic eligibility rules

The SSC CGL 2026 notice states that candidates for most posts need a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent. The Junior Statistical Officer and Statistical Investigator Grade-II have additional qualification conditions.

Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)

JSO requires a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, or a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level.

Statistical Investigator Grade-II

The 2026 notice lists specified bachelor's degree fields including Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Qualification cut-off

Final-year graduation candidates can apply, but the SSC CGL 2026 notice says the essential qualification must be possessed by 01.08.2026.

Important: This tool is a basic screening calculator. It does not automatically apply every post-specific age limit, category-based age relaxation, nationality rule, physical/medical requirement or other condition. Check the latest SSC notification before applying.
