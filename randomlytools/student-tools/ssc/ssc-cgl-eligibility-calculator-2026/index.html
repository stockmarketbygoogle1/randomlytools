Educational Qualification

Post / groupRequired qualification

Junior Statistical OfficerBachelor's Degree with either 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th level or Statistics as a subject at degree level
Statistical Investigator Grade-IIBachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science or Artificial Intelligence

JSO Qualification: What You Need

RouteCondition
Mathematics routeBachelor's Degree in any subject + at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level

Statistical Investigator Grade-II

The 2026 SSC notice specifies a bachelor's degree in one of the listed fields. Because this is a post-specific qualification, select the Statistical Investigator option in the calculator and confirm your degree against the official notice before applying.

Important: A general Bachelor's Degree is not automatically sufficient for every special CGL post. Always match your degree and subjects with the exact post requirement.

Final-Year Graduation Candidates

Candidates who have appeared in their final year can apply, but they must possess the essential qualification by 1 August 2026.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your DOB.
  2. Select the qualification type.
  3. If required, answer the JSO or Statistical Investigator condition.
  4. Select the applicable age band and confirm graduation.
  5. Click Check Eligibility and review the result.

What This Calculator Checks

Age

Compares your DOB with the selected standard age band using 1 August 2026.

Graduation

Checks whether you marked the required Bachelor's Degree as completed.

JSO

Checks the selected Mathematics/Statistics condition for JSO.

Statistical Investigator

Checks whether you confirmed a qualifying degree/field for this post.

What This Calculator Does Not Check

  • Nationality and citizenship conditions
  • Every post-specific requirement
  • Category-specific age relaxation
  • Physical or medical standards where applicable
  • Changes introduced by a later or revised notification

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the basic educational qualification for SSC CGL 2026?

For all other posts, a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent is required. Some posts have additional subject-specific qualifications. citeturn0search3

What qualification is required for JSO?

JSO requires a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, or a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level. citeturn0search3

What qualification is required for Statistical Investigator Grade-II?

The 2026 notice lists specified bachelor's degree fields including Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. citeturn0search3

Can final-year graduation students apply?

Yes, candidates who have appeared in their final year can apply, but they must possess the essential qualification by 1 August 2026. citeturn0search3
