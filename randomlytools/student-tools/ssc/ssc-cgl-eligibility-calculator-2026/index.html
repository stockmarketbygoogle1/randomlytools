SSC CGL Eligibility 2026

SSC CGL eligibility combines age, graduation and certain post-specific qualifications. This calculator provides a basic screening result and separates the special conditions for Junior Statistical Officer (JSO) and Statistical Investigator Grade-II.

Educational qualification

For all other posts, the SSC CGL 2026 notice requires a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent.

Junior Statistical Officer (JSO)

JSO requires a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with at least 60% marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level, or a Bachelor's Degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects at degree level.

Statistical Investigator Grade-II

The 2026 notice specifies bachelor's degree fields including Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Demography, Population Studies, Operation Research, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Technology, Computer Application, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Final-year graduation candidates

Candidates who have appeared in their final year can apply, but the essential qualification must be possessed by 1 August 2026.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your DOB.
  2. Select the standard age band for the post.
  3. Choose the qualification category.
  4. Confirm graduation and any special subject condition.
  5. Read the result and verify the full official notification.

Important limitations

The tool does not automatically apply category-specific age relaxation, nationality conditions, post-specific physical/medical requirements or every condition in the recruitment notice.
