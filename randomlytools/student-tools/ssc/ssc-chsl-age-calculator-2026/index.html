SSC CHSL Age Limit 2026

For SSC CHSL 2026, the standard age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026. For candidates applying under the standard age limit, the corresponding date-of-birth window is 2 August 1999 to 1 August 2008, with both boundary dates included.

SSC CHSL Age Relaxation 2026

The standard 18–27 year range is not the final upper-age limit for every eligible category. SSC provides upper-age relaxation under specified government rules. Common examples include 3 years for OBC and 5 years for SC/ST, while eligible PwBD and other specified groups can have different relaxation provisions.

Why Your Date of Birth Certificate Matters

For government recruitment, the date of birth entered in the application is not something that should be estimated from your current age. SSC uses the date of birth recorded in the prescribed educational certificate for age determination. A one-day difference can matter when you are close to an eligibility boundary.

Before applying, keep your matriculation or secondary examination certificate available and enter the DOB exactly as recorded there. If your documents contain a different date from the one you normally use elsewhere, follow the recruitment instructions and the accepted documentary record rather than guessing.

What If You Are Exactly 18 or 27 Years Old?

Being exactly 18 or exactly 27 does not automatically mean that you are outside the standard age range. What matters is the date-of-birth condition on the specified age-reckoning date. This is why the DOB window is included above.

For example, the latest standard DOB of 1 August 2008 reaches 18 years on 1 August 2026. Likewise, 2 August 1999 is the earliest standard DOB in the 18–27 window. Candidates near either boundary should use the calculator rather than relying on a simple birth-year subtraction.

SSC CHSL Age Calculator vs. Manual Age Calculation

Manual calculation can be enough for a rough estimate, but it can become confusing when the candidate is close to a cutoff date. A normal subtraction such as 2026 minus your birth year does not tell you whether your birthday has already occurred on the exact age-reckoning date. The calculator compares complete dates and therefore gives a more useful screening result.

For example, two candidates born in the same year can have different ages on 1 August depending on their birth month and day. That is why SSC eligibility is better checked using the full DOB rather than the birth year alone.

Important SSC CHSL Eligibility Checks

Age is only one part of recruitment eligibility. Before submitting an SSC CHSL application, candidates should also verify the educational qualification, nationality or citizenship conditions, post-specific requirements, category documents and any other conditions stated in the latest notification. Some posts can have additional educational requirements, so an age-eligible result should not be treated as a complete selection eligibility result.

Frequently Asked Questions About SSC CHSL Age

What is the SSC CHSL age limit for 2026?

The standard SSC CHSL 2026 age limit is 18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026. Eligible categories may receive upper-age relaxation according to the applicable rules.

What is the SSC CHSL 2026 DOB range?

For the standard 18–27 year age range, the DOB window is 2 August 1999 to 1 August 2008, inclusive.

Which date is used to calculate age?

This calculator uses 1 August 2026 as the default age-reckoning date for the SSC CHSL 2026 check.

Does OBC get age relaxation in SSC CHSL?

Eligible OBC candidates can receive the prescribed upper-age relaxation. The commonly applicable relaxation is 3 years, but candidates should confirm the category conditions in the current notification.

Does SC/ST get age relaxation?

Eligible SC/ST candidates generally receive 5 years of upper-age relaxation under the applicable SSC rules.

Can I use this calculator for final application decisions?

Use the calculator as a quick screening aid. Before applying, verify the latest official SSC CHSL notification, because the notification controls the final age, category, post and document requirements.
