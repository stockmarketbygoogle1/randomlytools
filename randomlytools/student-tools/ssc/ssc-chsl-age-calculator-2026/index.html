SSC CHSL Age Calculator 2026: Check Your Age by DOB

The SSC CHSL Age Calculator 2026 calculates your completed years, months and days on the selected date. The default date is 1 August 2026. It also checks whether your date of birth falls within the standard 18–27 year age window.

Quick facts: Standard age limit: 18–27 years · Age-reckoning date: 1 August 2026 · Standard DOB range: 2 August 1999 to 1 August 2008.

SSC CHSL Age Limit 2026

For the standard age category, candidates must be at least 18 and not more than 27 years old on the specified age-reckoning date. The corresponding DOB limits are shown below.

How to Use the SSC CHSL Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded on your matriculation or secondary certificate.
  2. Keep the calculation date as 1 August 2026 for the standard 2026 check.
  3. Select Check Age Eligibility.
  4. Read your exact age in years, months and days.
  5. Check the official notification separately if you need age relaxation or are applying for a post with special conditions.

Why Your DOB Certificate Matters

SSC age determination is based on the prescribed documentary record, not an estimated age or a guessed birth year. Enter the date exactly as it appears on your accepted educational certificate. Keep the document available before submitting the application.

What If You Are Exactly 18 or 27?

Being exactly 18 or 27 is not enough by itself to decide eligibility. The complete date of birth and the specified age-reckoning date matter. For example, 1 August 2008 reaches 18 years on 1 August 2026, while 2 August 2008 is still below 18 on that date.

Age Calculator vs. Manual Calculation

Subtracting 2026 from your birth year gives only a rough estimate. It does not show whether your birthday has occurred on the exact cutoff date. This calculator compares complete dates, making it more useful for candidates near the boundary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CHSL age limit for 2026?

The standard age limit covered by this calculator is 18 to 27 years on 1 August 2026.

What is the SSC CHSL 2026 DOB range?

The standard DOB window is 2 August 1999 to 1 August 2008, inclusive.

Does OBC get age relaxation?

Eligible OBC candidates commonly receive 3 years of upper-age relaxation, subject to the current notification and category conditions.

Does SC/ST get age relaxation?

Eligible SC/ST candidates commonly receive 5 years of upper-age relaxation under applicable rules.

Can this calculator make the final decision?

No. Use it as a screening aid and verify the latest official SSC notification before applying.
