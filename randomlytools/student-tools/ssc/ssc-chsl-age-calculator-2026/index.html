How to Use the SSC CHSL Age Calculator

  1. Enter the DOB shown on your matriculation/secondary certificate.
  2. Keep the calculation date at 1 August 2026.
  3. Click Check Age Eligibility.
  4. Compare the result with the standard DOB window and read the relaxation note.

Why the SSC CHSL Cutoff Date Matters

SSC uses the date of birth recorded in the required school certificate for age determination. Entering a different date can produce a misleading result.

SSC CHSL Age Relaxation

SSC rules can provide upper-age relaxation for eligible reserved categories and other specified groups. The exact relaxation depends on the applicable recruitment notice and candidate category. This calculator checks the standard age band and does not automatically apply every special relaxation.
