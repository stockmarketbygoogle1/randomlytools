SSC CHSL age limit 2026

For SSC CHSL 2026, the standard age requirement is 18 to 27 years as on 1 August 2026. For that standard range, the date-of-birth window is 2 August 1999 through 1 August 2008. Category-based age relaxation may apply under the recruitment rules.

How to use the SSC CHSL age calculator

Select your date of birth, keep the calculation date at 1 August 2026, and click Check Age Eligibility. The tool calculates your age in years, months and days and compares it with the standard 18–27 year range.

Important note about age relaxation

This page performs the standard age check. SC/ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and other eligible categories can have different upper-age rules. A calculator result should not replace the official notification.
