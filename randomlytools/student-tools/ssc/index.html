SSC Age Calculator 2026

An SSC age calculator helps you compare your date of birth with the age requirement for a particular SSC examination or post. Enter the DOB recorded in your matriculation or secondary certificate, select the relevant calculator, and review the result for the stated crucial date.

SSC Eligibility Calculator 2026

Age is only one part of SSC eligibility. Depending on the examination and post, eligibility can also involve educational qualification, nationality, experience, physical standards, language requirements and category-based age relaxation. Use the relevant eligibility calculator as a quick screening tool, then compare your details with the latest official SSC notice.

How to Use an SSC Calculator

  1. Choose the exact SSC exam and calculator.
  2. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded in your matriculation or secondary certificate.
  3. Select the post, qualification or option requested by the calculator.
  4. Review the result and the assumptions shown on the page.
  5. For an application decision, verify the latest official SSC notification and post-specific requirements.

Why the SSC Crucial Date Matters

The age-reckoning date can differ between SSC examinations. This is why an age calculator should always identify the examination and the date being used.

Important: Check the Official SSC Notification

RandomlyTools is an independent website and is not affiliated with the Staff Selection Commission. These calculators are designed for quick screening and convenience. Final eligibility depends on the latest official notification, the exact post, your category and the documents accepted by SSC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What SSC calculators are available on RandomlyTools?

The SSC section currently includes CHSL age and eligibility calculators, CGL age and eligibility calculators, and an SSC GD age calculator.

How does an SSC age calculator work?

You enter your date of birth and the calculator compares your age with the relevant exam or post rule and crucial date used by that tool.

Can I check SSC eligibility using my date of birth?

Age is one part of eligibility. The dedicated eligibility tools also consider relevant educational qualification and selected exam-specific conditions.

Why is the SSC age cutoff date important?

SSC notices use a specified crucial date for age reckoning. Your eligibility can change depending on the date used for the particular examination or post.

Are SSC age limits the same for every post?

No. Age limits can vary by examination and post, so check the requirement stated for the specific post in the latest SSC notification.

Does the calculator include age relaxation?

Some tools may show standard age conditions, but category-specific age relaxation should be checked against the relevant official SSC notice.

Is RandomlyTools an official SSC website?

No. RandomlyTools is an independent website. Use these calculators as screening aids and verify final eligibility from the official SSC notification.
