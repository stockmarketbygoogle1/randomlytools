SSC tools and calculators for 2026

This SSC hub brings related calculators into one place. Use age calculators to check DOB against the relevant SSC cutoff, and eligibility calculators to screen educational qualifications.

SSC CHSL tools

SSC CHSL Age Calculator 2026 checks the standard 18–27 age range on 1 August 2026. The SSC CHSL Eligibility Calculator 2026 adds the 12th qualification and selected DEO Science + Mathematics condition.

SSC CGL tools

SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026 supports the four standard post-wise age bands. The SSC CGL Eligibility Calculator 2026 checks graduation and special JSO and Statistical Investigator Grade-II qualifications.

SSC GD tool

SSC GD Age Calculator 2026 checks the standard 18–23 age range using 1 January 2026 as the age-reckoning date.

How to use these calculators

  1. Choose the exam and exact tool.
  2. Use your DOB as recorded in your matriculation/secondary certificate.
  3. Select the post or age band where requested.
  4. Read the result and assumptions.
  5. Verify final application rules in the latest SSC notice.

Important: eligibility is post-specific

SSC rules differ across exams and posts. Age relaxation, nationality, qualification, physical standards and other conditions can apply differently. These tools are quick screening aids, not final recruitment decisions.
