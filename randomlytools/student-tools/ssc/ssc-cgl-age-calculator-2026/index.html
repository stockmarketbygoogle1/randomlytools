SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026: Check Your Age by DOB

The SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026 calculates your exact age on 1 August 2026 and compares your date of birth with the age band selected for the SSC CGL post you want to check. SSC CGL does not have one common maximum age for every post, so selecting the correct post-wise age band is important.

How to Use the SSC CGL Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded in your matriculation or secondary examination certificate.
  2. Select the age band applicable to the SSC CGL post you are checking.
  3. Keep the calculation date at 1 August 2026.
  4. Click Check Age Eligibility to see your exact age and standard DOB-range result.

Why SSC CGL Has Different Age Limits

SSC CGL recruits for multiple posts, and the notification assigns different age requirements to different posts. That is why a single statement such as “SSC CGL maximum age is 32” can be incomplete. The correct check is post → age band → DOB → applicable relaxation.

The 2026 notification contains four standard age groups: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 years. citeturn0search14turn0search6

Important: Your DOB Must Match Your Certificate

SSC uses the date of birth entered in the application and the date recorded in the Matriculation/Secondary Examination Certificate for determining age. Because the calculator is only a screening tool, use the DOB shown on your official certificate when checking your result. citeturn0search12

What If Your Age Falls on a Boundary?

Do not judge eligibility only from your current age shown in years. SSC uses a fixed crucial date. A person can be 18, 20, 27, 30 or 32 during the year but still fall outside a particular post's DOB window. The calculator therefore compares the complete DOB against the selected range rather than using only the year of birth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL age limit in 2026?

It depends on the post. The 2026 notification uses 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 year age bands, calculated as on 1 August 2026. citeturn0search14

What is the DOB range for SSC CGL 18–32 posts?

The standard DOB range is 2 August 1994 to 1 August 2008, both dates inclusive. citeturn0search14

Can I use the 18–32 option for every SSC CGL post?

No. The age band must match the specific post you are checking. Different CGL posts can have different minimum and maximum ages.

Does the calculator include SC, ST, OBC or PwBD relaxation?

No. It checks the standard post-wise age band. If you are eligible for an age relaxation, verify the applicable rule in the official SSC notification before applying.
