SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026

The SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026 helps you calculate your exact age on 1 August 2026 and check whether your date of birth falls within the standard age range for the SSC CGL post you are considering.

Because SSC CGL has different age limits for different posts, select the correct age band in the calculator instead of assuming that one maximum age applies to every post.

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the age limit that applies to the SSC CGL post you want to check.
  3. Keep the calculation date as 1 August 2026.
  4. Click Check Age Eligibility.

The result shows your exact age, the selected age band and the corresponding standard DOB range.

SSC CGL 2026 DOB Range: What the Dates Mean

The DOB ranges above are the direct result of applying the minimum and maximum ages to the 1 August 2026 crucial date. The dates are inclusive. For example, for the 18–27 age band, 1 August 2008 is the latest eligible DOB, while 2 August 2008 falls one day outside the minimum-age requirement.

At the other end, 2 August 1999 is the earliest DOB for the 18–27 band. A DOB before that date is older than the standard upper-age limit for that band.

Why SSC CGL Has Different Age Limits

SSC CGL covers multiple posts, and the age requirement can vary from one post to another. Therefore, the useful sequence is:

Choose the post → check its age band → compare your DOB → check applicable relaxation.

This prevents a common mistake: treating the widest age band as if it applied to every SSC CGL post.

Which DOB Should You Enter?

Enter the date of birth exactly as recorded in your Matriculation / Secondary Examination Certificate. The DOB used in the application and the DOB recorded in the prescribed certificate are important for age determination. citeturn0search6

What If You Are Exactly 18, 27, 30 or 32?

Being exactly at an age limit is not enough by itself. SSC uses the fixed crucial date and the post-specific DOB window. That is why this calculator compares your complete date of birth with the selected range rather than looking only at your current age.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL age limit in 2026?

It depends on the post. The standard age bands are 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 years, with age reckoned as on 1 August 2026. citeturn0search3

What is the DOB range for an 18–32 post?

The standard DOB range is 2 August 1994 to 1 August 2008, both dates included. citeturn0search3

Can I select 18–32 for every SSC CGL post?

No. Select the age band that applies to the particular post you are checking.

Does this calculator apply SC, ST, OBC or PwBD relaxation?

No. It checks the standard post-wise age band. Applicable relaxation should be verified from the official notification.
