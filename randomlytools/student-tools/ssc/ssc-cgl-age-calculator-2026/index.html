SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026

SSC CGL does not use one age limit for every post. This calculator lets you choose the applicable standard age band and checks your DOB using 1 August 2026.

SSC CGL 2026 age bands and DOB ranges

Age limitEligible DOB range
18–27 years2 Aug 1999 – 1 Aug 2008
20–30 years2 Aug 1996 – 1 Aug 2006
18–30 years2 Aug 1996 – 1 Aug 2008
18–32 years2 Aug 1994 – 1 Aug 2008

Match your selected band to the exact post in the SSC vacancy/notification table before relying on the result.

How to use the SSC CGL age calculator

  1. Enter the DOB recorded in your matriculation/secondary certificate.
  2. Select the age limit for the post.
  3. Use 1 August 2026 as the calculation date.
  4. Click Check Age Eligibility.

Does SSC CGL have age relaxation?

Eligible categories may receive upper-age relaxation under SSC rules. This calculator shows the standard post-wise band and does not automatically add category relaxation.

Why post-wise checking matters

A candidate can fall inside one CGL age band but outside another. Selecting the actual post's age band avoids treating 18–32 as a universal CGL age limit.
