SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026: Check Your Age by DOB

The SSC CGL Age Calculator 2026 calculates your exact age in years, months and days on the selected date. The default age-reckoning date is 1 August 2026. It also checks your date of birth against the standard age band you select for the SSC CGL post you are checking.

Quick facts: Age-reckoning date: 1 August 2026 · Standard age bands: 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 years · DOB is checked against the selected post-wise band · Category relaxation is verified separately.

SSC CGL Age Limit 2026

SSC CGL does not have one common age limit for every post. The official 2026 notice lists different age bands, so select the band that applies to the post you want to check.

How to Use the SSC CGL Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded on your matriculation or secondary certificate.
  2. Select the age band that applies to the SSC CGL post you want to check.
  3. Keep the calculation date at 1 August 2026 for the 2026 check.
  4. Click Check Age Eligibility.
  5. Read your exact age and compare the displayed DOB range with your result.

Which DOB Should You Enter?

Enter the date of birth exactly as recorded in your Matriculation / Secondary Examination Certificate or the prescribed equivalent document. Do not enter an estimated birth date or change the date simply to fit an age limit.

Why Post-Wise Age Limits Matter

Using the widest age band for every SSC CGL post can give the wrong impression about eligibility. The calculator therefore asks you to select the applicable band before checking your DOB.

Simple check: Identify the post → find its age band in the notification → enter your DOB → check the applicable relaxation → verify the final eligibility conditions.

Age Calculator vs. Manual Calculation

Subtracting your birth year from 2026 gives only a rough age. It does not tell you whether your birthday has occurred on the exact age-reckoning date. This calculator compares the complete dates, which is especially useful when your DOB is close to the eligibility boundary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL age limit in 2026?

It depends on the post. The standard bands covered here are 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 years, with age reckoned as on 1 August 2026.

What is the SSC CGL 2026 DOB range for an 18–32 post?

The standard DOB window is 2 August 1994 to 1 August 2008, with both boundary dates included.

Does SSC CGL have the same age limit for every post?

No. The upper and, for some posts, the lower age requirement varies. Select the age band that applies to the particular post you are checking.

Does this calculator include OBC, SC/ST or PwBD age relaxation?

No. It checks the standard post-wise age band. Applicable category-specific relaxation should be verified from the current SSC notification.

Can this calculator make the final eligibility decision?

No. Use it as a screening aid and verify the latest official SSC notification and the requirements of the specific post before applying.
