SSC CGL Age Limit 2026

SSC CGL does not use one age limit for every post. Select the age band that applies to the post you are checking.

These standard age bands are used with the 1 August 2026 age-reckoning date. The correct band depends on the particular SSC CGL post.

How to Use the SSC CGL Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth exactly as recorded in your matriculation or secondary certificate.
  2. Select the age band that applies to the post you want to check.
  3. Keep the calculation date at 1 August 2026.
  4. Click Check Age Eligibility to see your exact age and standard DOB result.

Why Post-Wise Age Limits Matter

Simple check: Choose the post → identify its age band → compare your DOB → check applicable age relaxation → verify the official notification.

Using the widest age band for every post can give a misleading result. The calculator therefore asks you to select the age band before it checks your DOB.

Which DOB Should You Enter?

Enter the date of birth exactly as recorded in your Matriculation / Secondary Examination Certificate. The same DOB should be used consistently in the application and supporting records.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the SSC CGL age limit in 2026?

It depends on the post. The standard bands covered by this calculator are 18–27, 20–30, 18–30 and 18–32 years, using 1 August 2026 as the age-reckoning date.

What is the DOB range for an 18–32 post?

The standard DOB range is 2 August 1994 to 1 August 2008, with both boundary dates included.

Can I select 18–32 for every SSC CGL post?

No. Select the age band that applies to the particular post you are checking.

Does this calculator apply SC, ST, OBC or PwBD relaxation?

No. It checks the standard post-wise age band. Applicable relaxation should be verified from the official notification.
