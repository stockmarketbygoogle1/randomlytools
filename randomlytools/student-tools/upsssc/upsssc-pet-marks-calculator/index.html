Absolute, Normalised aur Percentile Score Kya Hai?

  • Absolute / raw score: Sahi answers se marks, minus negative marking.
  • Normalised score: Multiple shifts ki difficulty ko account karne ke liye commission ki process se calculate hota hai.
  • Percentile: Aapki relative performance ko candidates ke comparison mein dikhane wala score.

Ye tool sirf raw score calculate karta hai. Official formula ya candidate-specific data ke bina normalised score aur percentile ka andaza nahi dikhaya jayega.
