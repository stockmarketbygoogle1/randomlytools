Free student tools for exams and education

RandomlyTools Student Tools is a growing collection of practical calculators for students and competitive-exam aspirants. Tools are grouped by exam so you can reach the exact calculator quickly.

SSC exam calculators

Start with the SSC Tools & Calculators section for CHSL, CGL and GD age and eligibility checks. Each tool explains its cutoff date, qualification rule and calculation method.

What you can calculate

  • Age eligibility: compare your DOB with an exam's official age band and crucial date.
  • Eligibility: check basic educational qualification conditions.
  • Marks and percentage: calculate marks percentage and study metrics.
  • Attendance: find current attendance and target classes.

How to use an exam eligibility calculator

  1. Open the exam-specific calculator.
  2. Enter information exactly as shown on your certificates.
  3. Read the result with the assumptions and cutoff date.
  4. Verify final eligibility in the latest official notification.

Why the official notification still matters

Recruitment eligibility can depend on post, category, nationality, age relaxation, qualification cutoff and other conditions. A calculator is a screening aid, not a substitute for the official notification.
