Student Tools for Exams and Education

RandomlyTools Student Tools brings together focused calculators for students, competitive-exam aspirants and everyday study calculations. Instead of searching for separate formulas, you can open an exam-specific tool, enter the required details and review the result with the relevant assumptions.

What Can You Calculate?

  • Exam age eligibility: compare your date of birth with an exam's specified age range and crucial date.
  • Basic exam eligibility: check selected age and qualification conditions on dedicated exam pages.
  • Marks percentage: calculate percentage from obtained and maximum marks, including multiple-subject totals.
  • Attendance: calculate current attendance and work out target attendance requirements.

How to Use an Exam Calculator

  1. Open the calculator for the exact exam or calculation you need.
  2. Enter your information accurately, especially your date of birth and qualification details.
  3. Read the result together with the cutoff date, age band and other conditions shown on the page.
  4. For recruitment applications, verify the final rules in the latest official notification before submitting a form.

Why Check the Official Notification?

Recruitment eligibility can depend on the post, category, age relaxation, educational qualification, nationality, physical or medical standards and other conditions. Our calculators are designed as quick screening tools and do not replace the official notification or application instructions.
