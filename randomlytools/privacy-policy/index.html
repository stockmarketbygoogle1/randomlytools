Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

At RandomlyTools (accessible at https://randomlytools.pages.dev), user privacy is one of our primary principles. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected.

1. Client-Side Processing Guarantee

All core utility tools on RandomlyTools—including our random name generators, random number pickers, shop name generators, and username creators—run 100% on your local device within your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

We do not transmit, log, record, or store any of the numbers, filters, keywords, or results generated on our servers or external databases.

2. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require user accounts, logins, or passwords.
  • We do not collect credit card, banking, or payment information.
  • We do not record your generator inputs or search queries.

3. Log Files & Standard Web Traffic

Like most websites hosted on global content delivery networks (including Cloudflare), standard anonymous server access logs may temporarily record non-personally identifiable information, such as browser type, referring/exit pages, internet protocol (IP) addresses, date/time stamps, and approximate ISP locations. These logs are used solely for website maintenance, security mitigation (e.g., preventing DDoS attacks), and technical performance analytics.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

RandomlyTools does not use first-party tracking cookies to store user profiles. Third-party services, such as Google AdSense or analytics platforms, may use cookies, web beacons, or local storage to serve relevant advertisements based on past visits to this or other websites across the internet.

5. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites for documentation or informational purposes. We do not control the privacy practices or content of these external sites and encourage users to review their respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Information

RandomlyTools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take corrective action.

8. Contacting Us

If you have any questions or require additional details regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Page.
