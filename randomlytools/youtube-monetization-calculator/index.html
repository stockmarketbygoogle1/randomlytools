Current YouTube monetization requirements

YouTube has more than one Partner Program threshold. Some eligible channels can access selected fan-funding and shopping features at the lower tier, while full ad revenue sharing has a higher threshold. Requirements and feature availability can vary by country and product.

PathSubscriber requirementAdditional threshold
Early access to selected YPP features500 subscribersAt least 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, plus either 3,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 365 days or 3 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days.
Full ad revenue sharing1,000 subscribersEither 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 365 days or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days.

Always confirm the requirements shown in the official YouTube monetization requirements and the Earn tab in YouTube Studio before applying. The calculator is an independent planning aid, not an official YouTube tool.

How to use this YouTube monetization checker

  1. Open YouTube Studio and check your subscriber count and Earn tab progress.
  2. Enter qualified public watch hours from long-form public videos and qualifying archived livestreams. Watch time from Shorts in the Shorts Feed does not count toward the 4,000-hour path.
  3. Enter eligible public Shorts views for the most recent 90 days. Use the qualified figure shown in your analytics where available, not every raw playback.
  4. Choose Calculate my progress. The results show which numerical paths are met and the remaining gap for each threshold.
  5. Check policy, account and feature requirements in YouTube Studio. The final decision belongs to YouTube.

How the calculator works

The tool compares the figures you enter with thresholds published in YouTube's help documentation. For each path it calculates remaining subscribers, uploads, watch hours or Shorts views, and shows percentage progress toward each target. The watch-hour and Shorts-view routes are alternatives, not values that can be added together.

Important definitions

Qualified public watch hours

For the long-form route, eligible hours must come from public long-form videos and qualifying archived livestreams within the rolling 365-day period. Private, unlisted, deleted videos, ad campaigns and Shorts Feed watch time do not count toward this threshold.

Qualified Shorts views

The Shorts route uses eligible public Shorts views within the rolling 90-day period. YouTube's definition focuses on engaged views and excludes some ineligible activity. Use YouTube Studio for your authoritative count.

Does reaching the threshold guarantee monetization?

No. Thresholds allow a channel to apply or become eligible for a feature; YouTube still reviews the channel against its monetization policies and other account requirements.

Frequently asked questions

How many subscribers do I need to monetize a YouTube channel?

The full ad revenue path requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 365 days or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Selected early-access features may have a lower 500-subscriber path with extra activity requirements.

Do Shorts watch hours count toward 4,000 watch hours?

No. Watch time from views in the Shorts Feed does not count toward the 4,000 qualified public watch-hour route.

Can I use Shorts views instead of watch hours?

Yes. For full ad revenue eligibility, the Shorts route is an alternative to the long-form watch-hour route when the qualified views threshold is met. You do not add the two routes together.

Does this tool connect to my YouTube account?

No. Enter the numbers from YouTube Studio yourself. This tool does not ask for your login or channel authorization.

Why is my YouTube Studio progress different?

YouTube counts only eligible activity within defined rolling periods and applies its own rules. Use the Earn tab in YouTube Studio as the source of truth if your figures differ from this calculator.
