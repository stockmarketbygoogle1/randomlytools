How the Cricket Chase Calculator Works

A cricket chase calculator shows how many runs remain and the scoring rate required to reach a target. It is useful during a live chase or when planning a hypothetical chase scenario.

Runs Required = Target − Current Score

Required Run Rate = Runs Required ÷ Overs Remaining

Cricket Target Calculator

Enter the target score and current score to see the remaining runs. Add the overs remaining to calculate the required run rate.

Cricket Overs Notation

10.3 overs means 10 overs and 3 legal balls. It is not 10.3 decimal overs. This calculator converts cricket notation before calculating the required rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many runs are required to chase?

Subtract the current score from the target.

What is required run rate?

It is the average runs per over needed from the remaining overs to reach the target.

Does 10.3 mean 10.3 overs?

In cricket notation it means 10 overs and 3 legal balls, equal to 10.5 mathematical overs.
