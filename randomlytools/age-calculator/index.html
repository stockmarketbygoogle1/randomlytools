What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator by date of birth calculates your exact chronological age from your birth date and a selected date. Enter your date of birth above to find your age in completed years, months and days.

How to Calculate Age From Date of Birth

To calculate age from date of birth, compare your birth date with the date on which you want to know your age. First find the completed years, then the remaining months and days. The calculation must account for different month lengths and leap years.

For completed years, subtract the birth year from the target year. If the birthday has not occurred yet in the target year, subtract one year. The exact months-and-days result then uses the actual calendar dates rather than assuming every month has the same number of days.

Age Calculator Formula

Completed age = target year − birth year − 1 when the birthday has not yet occurred in the target year; otherwise, the full year difference is used.

Age is not simply the number of days divided by 365 because calendar years can contain 365 or 366 days and months have different lengths. For an exact calendar age, the calculator subtracts the birth year, month and day from the selected date while borrowing across months when necessary.

Calculate Age on a Specific Date

You can change the calculation date to any date in the future or past. This is useful when you need to know how old someone was on a particular date rather than today.

Age in Years, Months and Days

The result shows completed years plus the remaining months and days. For example, someone born on 15 January 2000 is 26 years old on 15 January 2026.

Total Days Since Birth

The calculator also gives the elapsed number of calendar days between the birth date and selected date. This can differ from simply multiplying completed years by 365 because leap years add extra days.

How Many Days Until My Next Birthday?

After entering a date of birth, the calculator identifies the next occurrence of the same month and day and counts the days remaining until that birthday.

Age Calculator Examples

  • 1 January 2000 to 1 January 2026 = 26 years.
  • 15 June 1995 to 15 June 2026 = 31 years.
  • 10 March 2010 to 20 September 2026 = 16 years, 6 months and 10 days.
