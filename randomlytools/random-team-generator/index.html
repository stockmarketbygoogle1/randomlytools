How to Use the Random Team Generator

  1. Add Your Participants: Paste or type one name per line into the text box. The participant counter updates live as you type, and blank lines are ignored automatically.
  2. Choose the Number of Teams: Pick anywhere from 2 to 10 teams using the quick-select pills.
  3. Click Generate Teams: The tool instantly shuffles your list and splits everyone into evenly sized, randomly assigned teams.
  4. Reshuffle or Copy: Not happy with the mix? Hit Reshuffle Teams for a fresh random split without re-typing names, or use Copy Results to grab a clean, ready-to-share list.

How Does the Random Team Generator Work?

This random team maker uses a proper Fisher-Yates shuffle (powered by your browser's cryptographic random number generator where available) to randomize your participant list, then distributes names evenly across the number of teams you choose. If the group doesn't divide evenly — say, 10 people into 3 teams — the extra participants are spread out fairly, so no team ever has more than one extra member than another. Every name is guaranteed a spot; nobody is left out.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a random team generator?

A random team generator is a free online tool that takes a list of names and randomly splits them into a set number of teams or groups, so nobody has to manually pick sides.

How does the random team generator divide people?

It shuffles your full list of names using a Fisher-Yates algorithm, then distributes the shuffled names evenly across your chosen number of teams. If the group doesn't divide evenly, the leftover members are spread out so team sizes never differ by more than one.

Can I create teams from a list of names?

Yes. Paste or type one name per line into the text box, choose your number of teams, and click Generate Teams.

Can I create uneven numbers of teams?

You can choose any number of teams from 2 to 10. If your participant count doesn't divide evenly by that number, the tool automatically balances team sizes so the difference is never more than one person.

Is the team generator free?

Yes, it's completely free with no signup, no limits, and no data sent to any server — everything runs locally in your browser.

Can I reshuffle the teams?

Yes. After generating teams, click Reshuffle Teams to get a fresh random arrangement of the same participant list without re-entering any names.

Can I use this for sports teams?

Absolutely. It works well for pickup games, practice squads, and tournament brackets where you need a fast, unbiased way to split players.

Can I use this for classrooms?

Yes, teachers commonly use it to randomly assign students to project groups or classroom activities.

Can I copy the generated teams?

Yes. Click Copy Results after generating teams to copy a clean, readable list of every team and its members to your clipboard.
