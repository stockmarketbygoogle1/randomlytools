How Much Does YouTube Pay in India?

There is no single fixed amount for 1,000 views in India. RPM changes with niche, viewer profile, video format, advertiser demand and season. This page uses a conservative planning model rather than promising a guaranteed rate.

YouTube RPM vs CPM in India

RPM is the creator-side revenue per 1,000 views and is the simplest input for an earnings estimate. CPM is an advertiser-side metric, so it should not simply be substituted for RPM.

How Many Views to Make ₹50,000 per Month?

At a ₹67 planning RPM, the simple RPM formula gives about 746,269 monthly views. Your actual requirement can be much higher or lower depending on your channel's real RPM.

FAQ

How much is 1 million YouTube views worth in India?

Using ₹67 RPM as a planning example, 1 million views would be about ₹67,000. Actual channel RPM can differ substantially.

Does Hindi content always have low RPM?

No. Language alone does not determine RPM. Niche, audience, viewer location, advertiser demand and content format all matter.
