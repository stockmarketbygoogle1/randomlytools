Estimate YouTube income in ₹ INR for an Indian audience. Enter monthly views and choose a niche to estimate daily, monthly and yearly creator revenue.

How Much Does YouTube Pay in India?

There is no single fixed YouTube payment rate in India. Two channels with the same views can earn different amounts because RPM changes with viewer location, niche, format, advertiser demand, season and monetization.

This India-specific page uses Indian rupees and a planning RPM so you can estimate revenue quickly. It is a planning tool, not an official YouTube payout calculator.

YouTube RPM Formula in India

Estimated revenue = views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. For example, 100,000 views at ₹67 RPM gives ₹6,700. If your actual RPM is ₹120, the same 100,000 views would give about ₹12,000.

YouTube RPM vs CPM in India

RPM is a creator-side revenue metric per 1,000 views. CPM is mainly an advertiser-side pricing metric. They are related but not interchangeable, so use RPM when estimating creator income.

How Many Views to Make ₹10,000, ₹50,000 or ₹1 Lakh?

At ₹67 RPM, approximately 149,254 views are needed for ₹10,000, 746,269 views for ₹50,000 and 1,492,537 views for ₹1,00,000. Your actual requirement changes with your real RPM.

Does Hindi Content Have a Lower RPM?

Language alone does not determine RPM. Niche, audience geography, format, watch behaviour and advertiser demand matter. An Indian creator can also have international viewers, which may change the channel's overall RPM.

YouTube Earnings by Niche in India

Finance, business, technology and professional topics can attract different advertiser demand from gaming, entertainment and music. The niche multipliers on this page are internal planning assumptions, not official YouTube rates.

Long-form vs Shorts in India

Shorts use a different monetization system and often have much lower revenue per 1,000 views than long-form videos. Use the YouTube Shorts Earnings Calculator for a separate Shorts estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does YouTube pay for 1,000 views in India?

There is no fixed amount. As a planning example, ₹67 RPM means ₹67 per 1,000 views, but actual RPM may be lower or higher.

How much can 1 million views earn in India?

At ₹67 RPM, 1 million views equals about ₹67,000. Use your own Studio RPM for a better estimate.

Does subscriber count determine YouTube earnings?

No. Subscribers can help generate views, but there is no fixed payment per subscriber. Views, monetization, audience and RPM are more directly relevant.

Is this an official YouTube earnings calculator?

No. RandomlyTools provides an independent estimate. Check YouTube Studio for your actual channel data.
