YouTube RPM Calculator 2026

Enter your YouTube revenue and total views to calculate your revenue per 1,000 views.

What Is YouTube RPM?

RPM means revenue per 1,000 views. It is a creator-side metric that helps answer a practical question: how much revenue did the channel generate for each 1,000 views? RPM can be more useful than CPM when planning creator income.

YouTube RPM Formula

RPM = Revenue ÷ Total Views × 1,000.

For example, if a channel generated ₹800 from 1,000,000 views, its RPM would be ₹0.80. If the same revenue came from 100,000 views, the RPM would be ₹8.00.

Why Your YouTube RPM Changes

RPM is not a permanent channel setting. It can change with audience country, niche, content format, seasonality, advertiser demand, monetization coverage, viewer behaviour and the mix of traffic your videos receive.

YouTube RPM vs CPM

CPM generally describes what advertisers pay for ad impressions or opportunities, while RPM is a creator-side revenue measure. A high CPM does not automatically mean the same value as RPM because they account for different parts of the monetization process.

What Is a Good YouTube RPM?

There is no single RPM that is good for every channel. Compare your current RPM with your own historical RPM and consider your niche and audience. Country and niche benchmarks can be useful for planning, but they should not replace your Studio data.

How RPM Affects YouTube Earnings

Once you know your RPM, estimated revenue can be calculated as views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. At ₹5 RPM, 100,000 views would be about ₹500. At ₹10 RPM, the same views would be about ₹1,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is YouTube RPM?

It is creator revenue per 1,000 views, calculated from revenue and total views.

How do I calculate YouTube RPM?

Divide revenue by total views and multiply by 1,000.

Is RPM the same as CPM?

No. RPM is creator-side; CPM is mainly advertiser-side.

What is a good RPM?

It depends on your audience, niche, format and channel. Your own Studio history is the best benchmark.

Can I use this calculator for Shorts?

You can calculate a mathematical RPM, but Shorts monetization works differently. Use the dedicated Shorts calculator for planning.
