What Is YouTube RPM?

RPM means revenue per 1,000 views. It is a creator-side metric and is more directly useful for estimating what a channel earns than advertiser-side CPM.

YouTube RPM Formula

RPM = Revenue ÷ Views × 1,000. For example, ₹800 earned from 1,000,000 views gives an RPM of ₹0.80.

RPM vs CPM

CPM describes advertiser-side pricing, while RPM is designed to show creator revenue per 1,000 views. They are related but are not interchangeable.
